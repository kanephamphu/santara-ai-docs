A **ticket** is anything that needs fixing or chasing: a broken air-conditioner, a guest
complaint, a supplier to call. They sit beside cleans on [Tasks](/daily/tasks/), and they have
their own screen when you want the whole list.

Tickets are internal. **Guests never see one**, even the ones opened from their own message.

![The ticket list: status, who it is assigned to, and the property it came from.](/screens/tickets.en.png)

## They open themselves

Most tickets are not created by a person. A guest message that reads as a complaint or a
maintenance problem opens one automatically, linked back to the thread and the property — so the
fix is tracked somewhere other than a conversation that scrolls away.

Three rules keep that from becoming noise:

- **One thread, one ticket, ever.** However many times the classification runs, a conversation
  that already produced a ticket never produces a second.
- **Only the last fortnight.** A thread nobody has touched in two weeks is history, not a job.
- **No flood on day one.** Connecting a year of message history does not open three hundred
  tickets — only the recent ones somebody can still act on.

You can also create one by hand, from this screen or by selecting dates on the
[calendar](/daily/calendar/).

## Working one

Open a ticket for the whole record:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Status** | Open through to done |
| **Assignee** | A teammate. They get a notification when it lands on them, and another when it is finished |
| **Property and room** | Carried from the message it came from |
| **The guest thread** | A link back to the conversation, so you can reply without losing the context |
| **Photos** | Evidence, before and after |
| **Schedule** | A date range — see below |

### Scheduling puts it on the calendar

Give a ticket a date range and **it is drawn on the [calendar](/daily/calendar/)** as a striped
block across those nights. A two-day repair then sits visibly against the bookings around it,
which is the point: you can see whether it collides with an arrival before you commit to it.

It does not block the nights on its own. If the room genuinely cannot be sold, close the dates in
the calendar as well.

### The AI resolution plan

Ask for a plan and you get a likely cause and a step-by-step fix, generated from the ticket, the
property and its history. It is a starting point for whoever does the work — particularly useful
for handing a job to someone who has not seen that building before.

It drafts. It does not order parts, message the guest, or close the ticket.

## Who can see them

Tickets follow the same [capability rules](/setup/team/) as everything else. A member without
ticket access is told so plainly rather than shown an empty list they would report as broken. If
you believe you should have access and do not, ask an owner or manager on the workspace.
