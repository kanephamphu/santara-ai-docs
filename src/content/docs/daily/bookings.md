**Reservations** lists every stay across every channel, plus any you add by hand.

![Reservations — every stay from every channel, grouped by when the guest arrives.](/screens/bookings.en.png)

## The list

Four tabs split the stays by where they are:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Stays that have not started yet |
| **In house** | Guests staying with you right now |
| **Past** | Stays that have ended |
| **Cancelled** | Bookings that were cancelled |

Upcoming stays are grouped **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Next 7 days** and **Later**, so the arrivals
that need you first are at the top. Tags on a row say **Arriving today**, **Departing today** or
**In house**.

Above the list:

- **Search** — by guest, property or channel.
- **Sort** — by check-in date, check-out date, revenue, guest name, property or status, ascending
  or descending.
- **Channel** — show one channel only.

## Opening a stay

Click any reservation and its details open beside the list:

- **Booking code** — the channel's reference, for looking the same booking up in an extranet.
- **Check-in and check-out**, with the times (**from 14:00**, **by 11:00**), and how far the stay
  has got — **Arrives in 3 days**, **Night 2 of 4**, **Checks out today**, **Stay completed**.
- **Property**, **Guests** and **Phone** (or **No phone number on file**).
- **Payout** — what you are paid for the stay. See [Money definitions](#money-definitions).
- **Notes**, where the stay has any.
- **Related** — **Open the message thread** and **See the clean**.

At the bottom: **Message guest**, and for stays you created in Santara AI, **Edit** and **Cancel
booking**. A stay that came from a channel is the channel's record — change or cancel it there,
and the change arrives here.

## Overbooked stays

When overlapping stays add up to more than a room's units, Santara AI flags it everywhere at once:

- a **red banner** at the top of Reservations, naming each room, the nights affected, and how many
  stays sit against how many units;
- an **Overbooked** badge on each stay involved;
- a **red bar** on the [calendar](/daily/calendar/);
- a notification in the bell, **Overbooked ·** followed by the room's name.

Move or cancel one of the stays on its channel before the guests arrive.

## Creating a booking by hand

For direct bookings taken over the phone, owner stays, or anything that did not come from a
channel: press **Add reservation** here, or select the nights on the [calendar](/daily/calendar/).

The **Guest name** field is also a guest picker. Type a name and pick someone under **Existing
guests** or **Recent guests** to add the stay to that guest's profile, or choose **Create new guest
“…”** for someone new. If a matching guest exists, the field tells you so — pick them rather
than creating a duplicate.

Saving pushes the availability change to your connected channels for the affected nights only — a
manual reservation closes those nights everywhere, exactly like one that arrived from Airbnb.

:::note[Manual stays carry less context]
A booking entered by hand has no channel calendar behind it, so gap detection and upsell timing for
that stay are only as current as the last edit. It is a fine way to block nights; it is a weaker
input to the automatic parts of the product.
:::

## Cancellations and changes

A cancellation on a channel arrives here within a minute, moves the stay to **Cancelled**, reopens
the nights, and produces a notification. A date change does the same. You do not need to touch the
calendar afterwards.

## Guests

A **guest** is a person, not a booking. Open a guest to see every stay they have had with you,
their messages, their reviews, and a score summarising how they have been to host. Repeat guests
are recognised across channels where the channel gives us enough to match on.

Use it before replying to a complaint from someone on their fourth stay — the answer is usually
different. See **[Guests](/daily/guests/)** for the score, the attention ordering and the trends.

## Money definitions

| Word | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **Gross** | What the guest paid, including the channel's commission |
| **Room revenue** | The accommodation portion, excluding fees |
| **Payout / net** | What the channel pays you. This is what Santara AI means by **revenue** |

A stay's details show the **Payout** only. See [Reports](/money/reports/) for the other figures.
