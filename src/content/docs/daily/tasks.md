**Tasks** is one screen for the work that has to happen in the physical world: cleans and
maintenance jobs.

![The cleaning board, grouped by day and then by building. Each card says whether it is a check-out clean or a prep, and who it is assigned to.](/screens/tasks.en.png)

## The cleaning board

Cleans are laid out in **day columns** — today, tomorrow, the day after. Each card is one turnover:
the room, the checkout time, the next arrival, and the cleaner assigned.

**Turnover tasks are created automatically the night before a checkout.** Nobody has to remember to
make them, and they are created for that date only — the board is a rolling few days, not a year of
pre-made rows.

For each task you can:

- **Assign a cleaner** — or leave the room's default assignment to do it.
- **Mark it done**, optionally with a photo as evidence.
- **Skip it**, with a reason, when a guest stays on or the room was not used.

### Default cleaner per room

Set the primary cleaner **per room** under the cleaning settings. There is no building-wide
default, deliberately: in practice one villa's rooms are covered by different people, and a
building default silently mis-assigns them.

### Cleaning clusters

A **cleaning cluster** is a group of properties one person covers — usually because they are within
a few minutes of each other. Set a property's cluster on its own page.

Clusters change two things: the board groups by them, and the [daily brief](/daily/) produces **one
WhatsApp message per cluster** listing only that cluster's jobs. That message is what you send to
the cleaner — it contains their work and nothing else about your estate.

### What a cleaner sees

A team member with the cleaner role sees **the cleans assigned to them**, and enough about the
property to find it. Not your bookings, not your revenue, not your door codes for other buildings.
See [Team and roles](/setup/team/).

## Tickets

The other half of this screen: complaints and maintenance, tracked from open to done. Most open
**by themselves** from guest messages that read as a complaint or a fault, and a scheduled ticket
is drawn on the [calendar](/daily/calendar/) so a two-day repair is visible against the bookings
around it.

Full detail — the automatic ones, the AI resolution plan, who can see them — on
**[Tickets](/daily/tickets/)**.

## Team chat

Beside tasks there is a **team chat** for the people working the property — the conversation that
otherwise happens in a WhatsApp group nobody can search later. It is separate from guest messages,
and guests cannot see it.
