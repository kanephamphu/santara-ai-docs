**Tasks** is one screen for the work that has to happen in the physical world. It has three tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Cleans** | The cleaning board — every turnover, by day or by building |
| **Issues** | Complaints and maintenance, tracked from To do to Done — see [Issues (tickets)](/daily/tickets/) |
| **Default cleaners** | Who cleans each room by default. Managers only |

![The cleaning board, grouped by day and then by building. Each card says whether it is a check-out clean or a prep, and who it is assigned to.](/screens/tasks.en.png)

## The cleaning board

**Turnover tasks are created automatically the night before a checkout.** Nobody has to remember to
make them, and they are created for that date only — the board is a rolling few days, not a year of
pre-made rows. Each card is one turnover: the room, the checkout time, the next arrival, and the
cleaner assigned.

The toolbar decides what you see:

- **When** — **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Earlier** or **All**.
- **Group by** — **By day** (a section per day, then buildings) or **By building** (buildings, then days).
- **Unassigned** — only the cleans nobody has been given yet.

Managers also get three counts at the top: **To clean today**, **Unassigned** and **Done today**.

For each clean you can:

- **Assign a cleaner** — or leave the room's default assignment to do it.
- **Mark done** when the room is finished. It reads **Undo** afterwards, in case it was pressed by
  mistake.
- **Add photo** — opens **Proof & notes**: attach photos (5 MB each) and notes, such as what you
  found or anything the host should know. Optional, but it settles questions later.
- **Take off schedule** (the **×** on the card) — when a guest stays on or the room was not used. No reason is asked for;
  the card then reads **Skipped · Undo**. Managers only: a cleaner does the work, they do not decide
  what work exists.

### Default cleaner per room

Set the primary cleaner **per room** on the **Default cleaners** tab. There is no building-wide
default, deliberately: in practice one villa's rooms are covered by different people, and a
building default silently mis-assigns them. New cleans for that room go straight to that person.

### Cleaners without an account

Not every cleaner will log in, and they do not have to. In any cleaner picker — on a clean, or on a
room's default — choose **Add a cleaner by name…**, type the **Cleaner's name** and, if you have
it, a **WhatsApp number (optional)**, then **Save cleaner**. They can be assigned straight away.

The picker then lists **Team members** and **Cleaners without an account** separately. A name-only
cleaner never sees the app; they get their work the way they always did, from the cluster's
shared plan below.

### Cleaning clusters

A **cleaning cluster** is a group of rooms one person covers — usually because they are within a
few minutes of each other, often across several buildings. Set it on each room's page under
**Cleaning group**: rooms with the same **Group name** belong to the same cluster.

Clusters shape the [daily brief](/daily/): its cleaning section is boxed per cluster, and each
cluster has a **Share** menu with that cluster's plan already written — its jobs and nothing else
about your estate. **Zalo** comes first, then **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, **Telegram**, **SMS** and
**Email**, plus a copy button. WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and Email open with the message filled in;
Zalo and Messenger cannot be pre-filled from a link, so the plan is copied for you to paste.
You can edit the message before sending.

### What a cleaner sees

A team member with the cleaner role sees **the cleans assigned to them**, and enough about the
property to find it. Not your bookings, not your revenue, not your door codes for other buildings.
See [Team and roles](/setup/team/).

## Tickets

The **Issues** tab: complaints and maintenance, tracked from **To do** through **In progress** to
**Done** or **Cancelled**, as a **Board** or a **List**, with priority, category, due date and
**Overdue** marking. Most open **by themselves** from guest messages that read as a complaint or a
fault.

Full detail — the automatic ones, the AI resolution plan, history, who can see them — on
**[Issues (tickets)](/daily/tickets/)**.

## Team chat

Team chat is not on this screen. It is the **Team** tab of [Messages](/daily/messages/) — the
conversation that otherwise happens in a WhatsApp group nobody can search later. Guests cannot
see it.
