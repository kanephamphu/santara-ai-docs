Every review guests left you, from every connected channel that passes reviews on, in one list.

![Reviews: how many need a reply, the scorecard with the overall rating, categories and each channel's own scale, then the Needs reply list with Reply with Santi and Write my own.](/screens/reviews.en.png)

The page reads top to bottom. One line says how many reviews **need a reply** and how many are
**below 4★**, with the period, property and room filters beside it. Under it sits the scorecard —
**Overall rating**, **By category** and **By channel**, explained under [Scores](#scores) — and then
the reviews themselves.

## Finding the ones that matter

The list has four tabs, each with its own count:

| Tab | What it shows | Opens sorted by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Needs reply** | Reviews you have not answered | **Oldest first** — the one waiting longest |
| **Below 4★** | Low ratings | **Lowest rating** |
| **Replied** | Reviews with your reply | **Newest** |
| **All** | Everything | **Newest** |

The page opens on **Needs reply** when anything is waiting, and on **All** when nothing is. You can
change the order with **Sort** (**Newest**, **Oldest first**, **Lowest rating**), narrow it by
**Period** (all time, the last 30 or 90 days, the last 12 months), by property and room, or
**Search reviews** for a guest's name or a word in the review.

On a wide screen the side panel lists your **Properties** with their average — select one to filter
the page — and **Review your guests**: the Airbnb guests still waiting for your review, with the days
left. Airbnb closes that window 14 days after checkout.

## Replying

Under a review without a reply, choose **Reply with Santi** or **Write my own**.

- **Reply with Santi** drafts a reply for you, marked as written by Santi. Make it **Warmer** or
  **Shorter**, or switch it to the **Guest's language**, and edit it as you like.
- **Write my own** opens an empty box.

**Send reply** publishes it back to the channel. The guest — and every future guest on that channel —
sees it where the review was left, so nothing is posted until you press it. Any member of the
workspace can reply — it is guest communication, not a management action.

## Scores

Channels score on different scales and different criteria. Santara AI stores them in a single shape,
so a Booking.com 8.6 and an Airbnb 4.3 can sit in the same average without one of them being
silently wrong.

- **Each review** shows the rating in the channel's own scale — stars, or a score out of 10 — so it
  matches what you see in the extranet.
- **Overall rating** at the top is shown **out of 5**, with a star breakdown and the change against
  the previous period.
- **By category** — cleanliness, accuracy, check-in, communication, location, value and so on — is
  also **out of 5**, with the weakest one called out. These are what to look at when the average
  moves and you want to know why.
- **By channel** shows each channel's average in its own scale, with your **Reply rate**.

## Reviewing a guest (Airbnb)

Airbnb lets a host review a guest, and Santara AI can publish that review for you. Press **Review
this guest** on their review. The form has:

- **Ratings** — one to five stars for **Cleanliness**, **Communication** and **Respect for house
  rules**. All three are required.
- **Would you host them again?** — Airbnb's recommendation flag, **Yes** or **No**.
- **Public review** — appears on the guest's Airbnb profile.
- **Private note to the guest (optional)** — only they see it.

Then **Review before publishing** shows you everything once more, and **Publish to Airbnb** sends it.

:::caution[This cannot be undone]
Publishing sends the review to Airbnb immediately. The public review appears on the guest's profile
and **there is no way to edit or delete it**. Airbnb reveals both reviews once you and the guest
have each submitted one.
:::

## Why replying matters more than it looks

On Airbnb, neither review is visible until both sides have written one, or the window closes. A
guest review sitting unreturned is a review your future guests cannot read yet. Doing them weekly —
it is in the [first-week rhythm](/start/first-week/) — is worth more than doing them well.

## Reviews on your booking site

Star ratings from your synced channels can be shown on your [direct booking
site](/setup/booking-site/) as a section. They are pulled from what has already synced; nothing is
written by hand.
