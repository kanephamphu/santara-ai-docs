Santi is on WhatsApp too, at **[+1 917-277-7669](https://wa.me/19172777669)**. It is the same Santi
as in [the Santi app](/help/santi-app/) and [on LINE](/help/santi-line/), with the same Santara AI
account behind it — you just talk to it where you already are.

Everyone uses their **own** Santara AI login, so Santi only ever sees and does what your role in the
workspace allows. A cleaner can start and finish their own cleans; they can't block dates or read
revenue.

:::note[Private chats only]
On WhatsApp, Santi works in your **private chat** with it. It doesn't take part in WhatsApp groups —
for a team group, use [LINE](/help/santi-line/), [Telegram](/help/santi-telegram/) or
[Zalo](/help/santi-zalo/).
:::

## Connect in two minutes

![Connecting Santi on WhatsApp in three steps: message the number, check the page and link, then ask anything.](/diagrams/santi-whatsapp-connect.en.svg)

1. Open **[wa.me/19172777669](https://wa.me/19172777669)** on your phone — or save
   **+1 917-277-7669** as a contact — and send **hi**.
2. Santi answers with **Sign in with Santara**. Tap it.
3. Sign in to Santara AI (you are often already signed in) and check the page: it names **your**
   WhatsApp number and **your** Santara AI account. Press **Link WhatsApp**.
4. Back in WhatsApp, Santi says you are linked — and answers the question you asked first.

:::caution[Only link your own WhatsApp]
If someone sends you a link to sign in, close it. The page says which WhatsApp number it will link —
if that isn't yours, don't press the button.
:::

The link works once and lasts 10 minutes; if it expires, send Santi any message for a new one. To
disconnect later, send **/unlink**.

## Ask anything

Write the way you would to a colleague — *"Who arrives tomorrow?"*, *"Draft a reply to Anna"*,
*"Is villa 2 free next weekend?"*. Santi reads your live Santara AI data, answers in your language
and keeps it short.

Santi reads text, photos and **voice messages**. Send a voice message (up to 2 minutes) and Santi
shows what it heard — *🎤 I heard: "…"* — then answers. A change you ask for by voice still comes
as a card you confirm by tapping; a spoken "ok" never confirms it. Stickers, videos and files are
ignored.

### Changes ask first

When you ask for a change — block dates, send a guest reply, assign a clean — Santi lists
**every detail** of it under the answer (what changes, from what to what) with **✅ Confirm** and
**Cancel**. Tap **✅ Confirm** once — or simply reply **OK** — and it's done. Reply **No** or tap
**Cancel** to drop it. Nothing changes before that, and only the person who asked can confirm. Big
changes, such as cancelling a booking, are confirmed in the Santi app instead.

When there are more than three choices, WhatsApp shows them as a list: tap the button under the
message, then pick one.

## Switch workspace

![Switching workspace in a chat: send /workspace, then tap another workspace.](/diagrams/santi-workspace-switch.en.svg)

If you work in more than one Santara AI workspace, send **/workspace**. Santi says which workspace
it's working in, with a button for each of your other workspaces — tap one to switch. You can also
type the name: **/workspace beach**. Everything Santi answers from then on is about that workspace.

The chat follows the same workspace as the Santi app, so switching in one switches the other.
**Add a workspace** opens Santara AI to connect another one.

## Cleaning: Start, Done and photos

Send **/jobs** — or ask *"what are my cleans today?"* — and you get today's jobs with a button for
each: **▶ Start** when you begin, **✅ Done** when it's finished. The host sees it in Santara AI
straight away. A cleaner sees only their own jobs.

**Photos:** send your photos to Santi. Santi asks **"Which job are these photos for?"**; pick the
job and they are attached to it in Santara AI. Send them in one go, up to 10 per job, and pick the
job within 30 minutes.

## Alerts

Linked people also get Santi's notices here: the morning summary, new bookings and cancellations,
and guests who have waited more than two hours for a reply.

:::note[The 24-hour window]
WhatsApp lets Santi message you only within **24 hours of your last message** to it. If you haven't
written to Santi today, a notice can't reach you on WhatsApp — open the Santi app, or send Santi any
message to open the window again.
:::

## If something doesn't work

- **Santi never answers.** Check the number is **+1 917-277-7669** and that you haven't blocked it.
- **The chat shows "Santara AI".** That's the same number — WhatsApp is still updating the name to
  *Santi by Santara AI*.
- **"You don't have access to this workspace."** Ask the host to add you to the workspace in
  Santara AI ([Team](/setup/team/)).
- **A morning summary didn't arrive.** See the 24-hour window above — send Santi any message.
- **The sign-in link expired.** Send Santi any message for a new one.
