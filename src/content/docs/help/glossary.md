**ADR** — average daily rate. Revenue divided by the nights that sold.

**AI Agents** — the crew of six agents Santi leads — Sunny, Lulu, Penny, Loop, Kiko and Bubbles — each
watching one part of the business. **AI Agents** in the sidebar opens their page, with **Needs you**
at the top. Agents only read. See [The AI Agents crew](/help/assistant/#the-ai-agents-crew).

**AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude)** — an outside AI app connected to your workspace, so you can ask it
about your calendar, bookings and cleans from where you already chat. Guest messages and bookings wait
for your yes; billing, settings, team and channels are never open to it. See
[Connect ChatGPT, Claude or Grok](/help/ai-assistants/).

**Auto-reply** — the Messages tab where Santi may answer simple guest questions on its own: **Off**,
**Practice** or **Live**. Off until an owner or admin switches it on; money, date changes, complaints
and emergencies are always left for you. See [Auto-reply](/daily/messages/#auto-reply).

**Capability** — a named permission such as `stay.read` or `pricing.write`. Roles are bundles of
capabilities; screens and API routes ask for the same ones. See [Team and roles](/setup/team/).

**Channel** — a place you sell: Airbnb, Booking.com, Agoda, Expedia and the rest of the catalogue, your own booking site.

**Cleaning cluster** — a group of rooms one cleaner covers, often across several buildings. Rooms with
the same **Cleaning group** name form one cluster. The daily brief boxes its cleaning section per
cluster, each with a **Share** menu (Zalo, WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, SMS, email) carrying that
cluster's plan only. See [Cleaning clusters](/daily/tasks/#cleaning-clusters).

**Cleaning group** — the setting on a room's page that puts it in a cleaning cluster. It is set per
room, not per building.

**Connection** — one linked channel account. A workspace can have many, including several on the
same channel.

**Daily brief** — the day written out: risks, arrivals, cleaning, revenue. Generated for the
workspace in the workspace's language, emailed at 06:00 by default, and shown on the **Brief** tab of
**Today**. See [Today and the daily brief](/daily/).

**Default cleaner** — the cleaner a room's new cleans go to automatically. Set per room on the
**Default cleaners** tab of Tasks; there is no building-wide default. See
[Default cleaner per room](/daily/tasks/#default-cleaner-per-room).

**Direct booking** — a booking through your own site.

**Go live** — the deliberate action that hands a listing's calendar control to Santara AI. See
[Going live](/channels/going-live/).

**Guest booking fee** — an optional fee on your [direct booking site](/setup/booking-site/#5-connect-stripe),
**paid by the guest** on top of the stay and shown as its own line. Off by default, and only on direct
bookings paid online at checkout — never on a booking request. Santara AI takes no commission on
channel bookings or upsells. See [Commission](/setup/billing/#commission).

**Guidebook** — the page a guest opens from the QR sticker in their room: house info, local places
and extras for anyone, the door code only for a guest who has confirmed their booking. See
[Guest guidebook and QR stickers](/setup/guidebook/).

**Gross** — what the guest paid, including the channel's commission.

**Handled** — a guest thread with nothing outstanding. One of the three thread statuses, with
**Needs reply** and **Waiting on guest**; **Mark handled** sets it, and the guest writing again undoes
it. See [One status per thread](/daily/messages/#one-status-per-thread).

**Health strip** — the strip at the top of every page when a channel connection or sync needs you:
one line per problem, each with **Fix**. It is not there when everything works. See
[Where problems show up](/channels/troubleshooting/#where-problems-show-up).

**Import channel data** — the button on a property that pulls the latest reservations, calendar,
messages and listing details from the channel. Where the channel holds a value, it replaces the
property's. See [Syncing from the channel](/setup/properties/#syncing-from-the-channel).

**Issues** — the tab of **Tasks** where tickets live: complaints and maintenance, from **To do** to
**Done**. There is no separate Tickets item in the sidebar. See [Issues (tickets)](/daily/tickets/).

**Late** — see **Waiting / Late**.

**Listing** — one thing on a channel, mapped one-to-one to a Santara AI room.

**Manual price** — a nightly rate you typed. Protected from the pricing engine unless you allow
overwriting.

**Mapping** — the link between a channel listing and a Santara AI room.

**Needs you** — the filter the inbox opens on: the guest threads waiting for something from you. The
AI Agents page has a **Needs you** list too. In the inbox, **Mark all handled** clears it in one go. See
[What the list shows](/daily/messages/#what-the-list-shows).

**No room assigned** — a greyed calendar row under a property, holding stays that sit on none of its
rooms. It appears only when there is such a stay; a channel booking there usually means a listing is
mapped wrongly. See [Reading it](/daily/calendar/#reading-it).

**Notifications** — the bell. Sixteen kinds, across bookings (including **Overbooked**), cleaning,
tickets, channels, guest requests and AI assistants, each sent to whoever can act on it. See
[Notifications](/setup/workspace/#notifications).

**Occupancy** — nights sold divided by nights available.

**Overbooked** — a room holding more stays than it has units on some nights, so one stay has to move.
Shown as a red banner on Reservations, a badge on each stay, a red bar on the calendar and a bell
notification. See [Overbooked stays](/daily/bookings/#overbooked-stays).

**Owner statement** — the one screen of Reports: for a month, gross booking revenue, each channel's
fee, expenses, and the **Owner net** left over. See [The owner statement](/money/reports/#the-owner-statement).

**Payout / net** — what the channel actually pays you. This is what Santara AI means by **revenue**.

**Property** — a building or address. Holds rooms.

**Rate plan** — a price for a room plus its rules: minimum stay, guests included, extra-guest
price, cancellation terms.

**Reply-time target** — how fast a guest should hear back at that moment of the stay: 10 minutes on
check-in day, 30 in house, 60 for an enquiry, and so on. Passing it turns **Waiting** into **Late**.
See [How long a guest has been waiting](/daily/messages/#how-long-a-guest-has-been-waiting).

**RevPAN** — revenue per available night: revenue divided by every night you had to sell. The
number to judge pricing on.

**Room** — a bookable unit. What guests book, what channels map to, what is billed.

**Rule set** — a collection of pricing rules: seasons, day-of-week premiums, events. Shared across
listings.

**Santi** — the AI assistant inside Santara AI, beside the bell on every screen. It reads your
workspace, leads the AI Agents crew, and changes something only after you press **Confirm**. See
[Ask Santi](/help/assistant/).

**Stay** — a reservation. Also called a booking. They are listed on **Reservations**.

**Ticket** — a tracked issue: a complaint, a repair, a job. Lives on **Tasks → Issues**. Internal;
guests never see one. See [Issues (tickets)](/daily/tickets/).

**Turnover** — the clean between a checkout and the next arrival. Created automatically the night
before.

**Unit count** — how many identical units a room represents. A hard cap on availability.

**Upsell** — an extra night, a late checkout or an early arrival sold to a guest already staying.

**Waiting / Late** — the label on a thread waiting for your reply: **Waiting 12 min**, then
**Late · 40 min** once it passes the [reply-time target](/daily/messages/#how-long-a-guest-has-been-waiting)
for that moment of the stay.

**Workspace** — one portfolio: its properties, team, billing and booking site. Nothing is shared
between workspaces.
