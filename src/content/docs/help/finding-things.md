## Search — <kbd>⌘K</kbd>

Press <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>) anywhere in the product. Search covers four
things:

| | Find it by |
| --- | --- |
| **Reservations** | Guest name, confirmation code, dates |
| **Guests** | Name, email, phone |
| **Properties** | Name, city |
| **Rooms** | Name, or the property they are in |

Results carry enough context to pick the right one without opening it — a reservation shows its
dates and status, a room shows its building. Dates and statuses are shown in **your** language,
not the database's.

It is the fastest way into a specific stay when a guest phones. Confirmation code, enter, done —
faster than the [bookings](/daily/bookings/) feed, which is built for scanning rather than
looking one thing up.

## The walkthrough

A new workspace offers **"Show me around"** — around twenty short steps that point at each part of
the product and say what it is for, grouped into chapters: getting oriented, every day, every week
or so, going live, and where to get help.

- **Stop whenever you like.** It picks up where you left off, and the resume pill tells you which
  chapter you are in rather than "step 11".
- **"Don't show me this again"** means it. You will not be asked a second time.
- **Restart it** from the setup steps on [Properties](/setup/properties/) or
  [Settings → Channels](/channels/).

It adapts to you. Steps for screens your role cannot open are skipped rather than walking you into
a refusal, and steps for setup you have already done do not appear. Parts of the product your plan
does not include are still shown, with a lock — you can see what is there without being sold at
mid-sentence.

:::note[It needs a wide screen]
The walkthrough points at things, and on a phone there is nothing to point at — the layout is
different. Open Santara on a laptop for it.
:::

## The setup checklist

Separate from the walkthrough, and the thing to actually follow first: three steps from an empty
workspace to live on your channels, tracked as you go.

1. [Connect your channel account](/channels/)
2. [Import your listings](/channels/airbnb/)
3. [Check prices and details](/setup/properties/)

It sits at the top of Properties and Settings → Channels until it is done. Not selling on Airbnb or
Booking.com? It offers the manual path instead — see
[Properties and rooms](/setup/properties/#creating-one-by-hand).

## When you still cannot find it

- Ask the [assistant](/help/assistant/) — it can read your workspace, so "which room is Mai Nguyen
  in" is a fair question.
- Search these docs, top of any page.
- [Contact support](/help/support/) from inside the product.
