Santi is on LINE too, as **[Santi by Santara AI](https://line.me/R/ti/p/@313bjizp)** (LINE ID
**@313bjizp**). It is the same Santi as in [the Santi app](/help/santi-app/) and
[on Telegram](/help/santi-telegram/), with the same Santara AI account behind it — you just talk to
it where your team already talks.

Everyone uses their **own** Santara AI login, so Santi only ever sees and does what your role in the
workspace allows. A cleaner can start and finish their own cleans; they can't block dates or read
revenue.

## Connect in two minutes

![Connecting Santi on LINE in three steps: add Santi as a friend, check the page and link, then ask anything.](/diagrams/santi-line-connect.en.svg)

1. Open **[line.me/R/ti/p/@313bjizp](https://line.me/R/ti/p/@313bjizp)** on your phone — or in LINE,
   go to **Add friends → Search**, search the ID **@313bjizp** — and tap **Add**.
2. Santi greets you with **Sign in with Santara**. (Already added Santi earlier? Send it any message,
   such as *"What's on today?"*.)
3. Tap it. It opens in your phone's own browser (Safari or Chrome) — sign in to Santara AI and check
   the page: it names **your** LINE account and **your** Santara AI account. Press **Link LINE**.
4. Back in LINE, Santi says you are linked — and answers the question you asked first.

:::caution[Only link your own LINE]
If someone sends you a link to sign in, close it. The page says which LINE account it will link —
if that isn't you, don't press the button.
:::

The link works once and lasts 10 minutes; if it expires, send Santi any message for a new one. To
disconnect later, send **/unlink**.

## Ask anything

Write the way you would to a colleague — *"Who arrives tomorrow?"*, *"Draft a reply to Anna"*,
*"Is villa 2 free next weekend?"*. Santi reads your live Santara AI data, answers in your language
and keeps it short. While it works you see LINE's "…" indicator.

Santi reads text, photos and **voice messages**. Send a voice message (up to 2 minutes) and Santi
shows what it heard — *🎤 I heard: "…"* — then answers. A change you ask for by voice still comes
as a card you confirm by tapping; a spoken "ok" never confirms it. Stickers, videos and files are
ignored.

### Changes ask first

When you ask for a change — block dates, send a guest reply, assign a clean — Santi lists
**every detail** of it under the answer (what changes, from what to what) with **✅ Confirm** and
**Cancel**. Tap **✅ Confirm** once — or simply reply **OK** — and it's done. Reply **No** or tap
**Cancel** to drop it. Nothing changes before that, and only the person who asked can confirm. Big
changes, such as cancelling a booking, are confirmed in the Santi app instead.

LINE can't remove buttons from a message once it's sent, so old buttons stay in the chat. Tapping
one again is safe: a confirmation works only once, and Santi checks a job before changing it.

## Switch workspace

![Switching workspace in a chat: send /workspace, then tap another workspace.](/diagrams/santi-workspace-switch.en.svg)

If you work in more than one Santara AI workspace, send **/workspace** in your private chat with
Santi. Santi says which workspace it's working in, with a button for each of your other workspaces —
tap one to switch. You can also type the name: **/workspace beach**. The chat follows the same
workspace as the Santi app, so switching in one switches the other. In a group, the workspace is
the one connected with **/use**.

## Cleaning: Start, Done and photos

Send **/jobs** — or ask *"what are my cleans today?"* — and you get today's jobs with a button for
each:

- **▶ Start** when you begin a clean.
- **✅ Done** when it's finished. The host sees it in Santara AI straight away.

A cleaner sees only their own jobs. A host or co-host sees everyone's (with more than 8 open jobs
the list comes without buttons — open the job in Santara AI instead). An old **▶ Start** on a job
that's already finished, or from another day's list, changes nothing — Santi says so. Send
**/jobs** for today's list.

**Photos:** send your photos to Santi in your **private chat** with it. Santi asks **"Which job are
these photos for?"**; tap the job and they are attached to it in Santara AI, then it offers
**✅ Done**. Send them in one go (an album counts once), up to 10 per job, and pick the job within
30 minutes.

## Your team group

![Adding Santi to a LINE group: invite Santi by Santara AI, a host sends /use, then mention Santi — here with /jobs and its Start / Done buttons.](/diagrams/santi-line-group.en.svg)

1. In the group, tap **≡** (top right) → **Invite**, pick **Santi by Santara AI** and tap
   **Invite**. You need Santi as a friend first.
2. A **host, admin or co-host** sends **/use**. The group now works with that workspace.
3. Anyone in the group talks to Santi by typing **@**, picking **Santi by Santara AI** from the list
   and writing the question — or by sending **/jobs**. Messages without the mention are left alone.

Everyone in a group reads Santi's answers, so a normal team group gets only **team things** —
cleaning, tickets and dates. Revenue, payouts and guest messages are answered **in a private chat**
with Santi instead. A group for managers only can be set up with **/use managers**.

Each person still has to link their own LINE the first time. Santi sends them the sign-in
privately — so they need Santi as a friend too; if they haven't added it, Santi shows them the
add-friend link.

## Alerts

Linked people also get Santi's notices in their **private** chat (never in a group): the morning
summary, new bookings and cancellations, and guests who have waited more than two hours for a
reply. Each comes with a link that opens Santi with the question already asked.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| **/jobs** | Today's cleaning with ▶ Start / ✅ Done |
| **/use** | Connect this group to your workspace (hosts, admins, co-hosts) |
| **/help** | What Santi can do here |
| **/workspace** | See your workspace, or switch with a button or **/workspace** *name* (private chat) |
| **/unlink** | Disconnect your LINE account |

## If something doesn't work

- **Santi doesn't answer in the group.** Type **@** and pick **Santi by Santara AI** from the list —
  typing the name by hand doesn't count as a mention. If it says the group isn't connected, a host
  or co-host sends **/use**.
- **Santi never answers at all.** Check you added **@313bjizp** as a friend and didn't block it.
- **The sign-in page loops or forgets you.** Make sure it opened in Safari or Chrome, not inside
  LINE. If it opened inside LINE, use **⋯ → Open in browser**.
- **"You don't have access to this workspace."** Ask the host to add you to the workspace in
  Santara AI ([Team](/setup/team/)).
- **A button says you can't change that job.** It's assigned to someone else — ask the host to
  assign it to you.
- **The sign-in link expired.** Send Santi any message for a new one.
