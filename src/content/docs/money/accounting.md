---
title: Accounting is now part of Reports
description: The separate Accounting screen was retired. Costs and owner statements now live on one screen, Reports.
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

There is no separate Accounting screen any more. Costs, owner statements and the CSV for your
accountant are all on **[Reports](/money/reports/)** — one owner statement a month, with the
expenses that were subtracted listed right underneath it.

Where things went:

| You used to… | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Log a cleaning, utility or repair cost | **Add expense** on Reports — [Adding an expense](/money/reports/#adding-an-expense) |
| Split one bill across several listings | **Also covers** — [Shared bills](/money/reports/#shared-bills) |
| Produce an owner statement for a period | The [owner statement](/money/reports/#the-owner-statement), for a month or any range |
| Export for your accountant | [Download CSV](/money/reports/#download-csv) |

The statement shows gross, each channel's fee, expenses and the owner net. It does not apply a
management-fee split — take your share outside Santara AI, from the CSV.
