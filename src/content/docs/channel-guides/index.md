Every channel connects through the same setup in Santara AI — choose property, connect and map,
import ([Connect another channel](/channels/other-channels/)). What differs is the work **on the
channel's side** first, the **ID or login** it gives you, and how its **rooms and rates map**. Each
guide below covers one channel.


## Where you connect a channel

Open **Settings → Channels → Add a channel** and press **Connect** on the channel.

![Settings → Channels → Add a channel: every channel you can connect.](/screens/channel-guides/catalogue.en.png)

Then pick the property the channel is for. Step 2 shows that channel's own setup fields — each guide has a screenshot of them.

![Step 1: choose the property. A channel connects to one property at a time, with its rooms.](/screens/channel-guides/property.en.png)

## Before any channel

- **You need your own contract with the channel**, with the property's details and photos already in
  its extranet. Santara AI cannot create a listing on a channel for you.
- **The channel has to allow the connection first.** In its extranet, or by asking its support,
  choose **Channex** as your channel manager or connectivity provider. Channex is Santara AI's
  connectivity partner — it is the name the channels know us by.
- **Remove any old channel manager or iCal link** for that property on the channel. Two systems
  updating the same calendar overwrite each other.

## Two mapping styles

- **Multi-occupancy** — one price per guest count (1 guest, 2 guests, …). If you only have one
  price for the room, map that same rate to every guest count.
- **Per room** — one price for the room, whoever stays. Map your full-occupancy rate.

## Guest messages

Replying to guests from Santara AI works on **Airbnb**, **Booking.com** and **Expedia**. On every
other channel, answer guests in the channel's own inbox.

## Still stuck?

- **Test connection fails** — check the ID, then ask the channel's support to confirm the property
  is connected to Channex and that no other channel manager is.
- **Rooms or rates missing on the mapping screen** — they are not set up in the channel's extranet
  yet, or the channel has not finished its side. Add them there and reopen the setup.
- More fixes: [Channel troubleshooting](/channels/troubleshooting/).
