These channels are in **Settings → Channels → Add a channel** but have no guide of their own. They all connect the same way: ask the channel to connect your property to **Channex** (Santara AI's connectivity partner), then enter what it gives you — see [Connect another channel](/channels/other-channels/) for the steps.

Most also show **Send Property Notification** (tick it and a **Property Email** field appears) and some a **Min Stay Type** (usually Arrival). Both are optional and left out of the table.

## Channels that open the embedded setup screens

On these, step 2 opens the channel manager's own setup screens inside Santara AI. Fill them in there and press **I'm done**.

| Channel | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| Ascend Travel | **Hotel ID**, **Booking Amount** |
| More.com | Nothing — choose the rate plans to send |
| WeSpeak | Nothing — choose the rate plans to send |

## Channels with nothing to enter

Nothing to sign in with — pick the rate plans the channel should receive.

| Channel | What you enter |
| --- | --- |
| HotelPoint | Nothing — choose the rate plans to send |
| Open Shopping | **Property Slug** |
| Reserva | **Agency ID** |

Not sure what a field means? Ask the channel's support for the value they issued when they connected Channex.
