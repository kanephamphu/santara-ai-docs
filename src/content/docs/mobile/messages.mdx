The **Messages** tab is the central hub for all communications in your workspace. You can toggle between two flows: **Guest messages** and **Team** chat.

The tab appears only if your role can read guest messages, so cleaners do not see it — see [Why you see fewer tabs](/mobile/getting-started/#6-why-you-see-fewer-tabs).

## 1. Guest Messages

All channels (Airbnb, Booking.com, direct bookings…) are consolidated into one list so you never miss a message.

- **Filters:** **Needs you**, **Unread**, **Today** and **All**. **Needs you** is the default — conversations still waiting on a reply.
- **Channel Identification:** A small icon next to the guest's name shows whether they came from Airbnb, Booking.com or elsewhere.
- **Preview / Summary:** Read a preview or summary of the last message right from the list.
- **Unread count:** An unread conversation is in bold, with a pill showing how many messages are unread (**99+** above 99). High-priority conversations have a red edge.
- **Pin:** Tap the pin icon to keep a conversation at the top (**Pin to top** / **Unpin**).
- **Open:** Tap a conversation to read it in full and send messages, photos or files from your phone.

![Guest chat flow](/screens/en/guest.en.png)

### Suggested Reply
Tap **Suggest** above the reply box and the AI reads the conversation (a WiFi question, how to open the door…) and writes a **Suggested reply**:

- Type what you want into **Tell the AI what to say** (for example, offer a late checkout) and tap **Generate reply** for a new version.
- Tap **Use this reply** to put it into the reply box, where you can edit it before sending. If you had already typed something, the app asks before replacing it.
- Tap **×** (**Dismiss suggested reply**) to throw it away.

Nothing is sent until you press send.

### Mark Handled
Once a guest's issue is resolved, tap **Mark handled** at the top of the conversation. It leaves **Needs you**, so that list only holds what still waits on you. A handled conversation shows **Needs reply** instead, to put it back.

## 2. Team Chat

Your team (managers and staff) can chat one-to-one or in groups without an external app.

- **Create New:** Tap **New** and choose one teammate for a direct message, or several for a group.
- **Quick Filters:** **All**, **Groups**, **Direct** and **Unread**.
- **Search:** Type into the search bar to find a group chat or a member's name.

![Team chat](/screens/en/team_chat.en.png)

### Add to Checklist
If a staff member writes *"Toilet broken in room 102"*, **long-press** the message and choose **Add to checklist**. The message becomes an item on that conversation's **Checklist** (under **Conversation info**), so it is not lost in the scroll.

### Seen By
Under a message you sent, the app shows **Seen by** and the names of who has read it, so you know a staff member has received an instruction.
