The header at the top and the menu behind the **More** tab are where you reach search, your account and the settings of Santara AI Mobile.

## 1. Global Search (Workspace Search)

Tap the **Search** icon (magnifying glass) in the header. *Search by guest name, confirmation code, property or room.* Type at least 2 characters; results are grouped into **Reservations**, **Guests**, **Properties** and **Rooms**, and your recent searches are kept for next time.

## 2. The Menu (More)

Tap the **More** tab to slide out the menu:

### Switch Workspace
If you work in several workspaces (for example, for more than one host), tap the current workspace name at the top of the menu and pick another. All data reloads straight away. The menu also shows your role in the current workspace.

### Navigation
The menu lists the screens your role can open — **Today**, **Messages**, **Tasks**, **Calendar** under **Every day**, and **Settings** under **Setup**.

### Appearance
At the foot of the menu, tap **Dark mode** (or **Light mode** while dark) to switch the app's look.

### Your Account and Sign Out
Tap **Personal account** at the foot of the menu, then:

- **Personal settings** — your **Account preferences** (profile photo, name, language, timezone), and **Change password**. Accounts are closed on the web — see [Deleting your account](/mobile/getting-started/#8-deleting-your-account).
- **Sign out** — logs you out of the app after a confirmation.

## 3. Detailed Settings

From the menu, open **Settings**. It has two tabs:

### Workspace
Change the workspace name and logo (only hosts or administrators can). The changes apply to everyone in the workspace immediately. Below that are the workspace's **Operating preferences**, including **Display currency** (USD, IDR or VND) used for revenue figures.

![Workspace Settings](/screens/en/workspace.en.png)

### Message Templates
Set up pre-written replies (check-in instructions, house rules, WiFi password…). Create a template once here, and you and your staff can insert it from **Templates** when replying to a guest instead of typing from scratch.

![Message Templates](/screens/en/template.en.png)

## 4. Santi and its menu

The **Santara | Santi** switch at the top of the screen opens **Santi**, the AI agent, in place of your usual tabs. Santi has its own menu — tap **⋯** next to **Voice** — for its language, workspaces, what it remembers, notifications, **Clear conversation** and signing out of Santi. See [The Santi menu](/mobile/santi/#7-the-santi-menu).
