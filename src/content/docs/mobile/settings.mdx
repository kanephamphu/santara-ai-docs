The menu behind the **More** tab is where you reach your account, the screens your role can open and the settings of Santara AI Mobile.

## 1. The Menu (More)

Tap the **More** tab to slide out the menu:

### Switch Workspace
If you work in several workspaces (for example, for more than one host), tap the current workspace name at the top of the menu and pick another. All data reloads straight away. The menu also shows your role in the current workspace.

### Navigation
The menu lists the screens your role can open — **Today**, **Messages**, **Tasks**, **Calendar** and **Reservations** under **Every day**, and **Settings** under **Setup**.

### Appearance
At the foot of the menu, tap **Dark mode** (or **Light mode** while dark) to switch the app's look.

### Your Account and Sign Out
Tap **Personal account** at the foot of the menu, then:

- **Personal settings** — your **Account preferences** (profile photo, name, language, timezone) and **Change password**. Accounts are closed on the web, not in the app: [santara.ai/delete-account/](https://www.santara.ai/delete-account/).
- **Sign out** — logs you out of the app after a confirmation.

## 2. Detailed Settings

From the menu, open **Settings** — *Manage your workspace and operating preferences.* It has two tabs: **Workspace** and **Message templates**.

### Workspace

- **Workspace details** — the **Current workspace** name, which you can change and keep with **Save name**, and **Your role in this workspace**. Only hosts or administrators can rename the workspace, and the change applies to everyone in it straight away.
- **Workspace avatar** — the logo shown for this workspace in the menu and the workspace switcher. Tap **Upload image** (PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF, up to 5 MB).
- **Operating preferences** — how the app prioritises briefs, messages and upsell suggestions for the whole workspace, including **Display currency** (USD, IDR or VND) used for revenue figures.

![The Workspace tab: workspace name, your role, avatar and operating preferences](/screens/en/workspace.en.png)

### Message templates

Pre-written replies — check-in instructions, house rules, a review request, a late checkout offer. Create a template once here, and you and your staff can insert it from **Templates** when replying to a guest instead of typing from scratch.

- **New template** writes one from scratch; **Import defaults** adds Santara's starter set to the workspace.
- **Saved templates** lists them with a count. Each shows its category (**Check-in**, **Check-out**, **Upsell**, **Review**, **Issue**, **Operations** or **General**), a preview of the text, and the parameters it uses, such as `{guest_name}` or `{property_name}`, which are filled in from the booking when you send it.
- **Edit** or **Delete** a template from its card.

![The Message templates tab: saved templates with their category, parameters, Edit and Delete](/screens/en/template.en.png)

## 3. Santi and its menu

The **Santara | Santi** switch at the top of the screen opens **Santi**, the AI agent, in place of your usual tabs. Santi has its own menu — tap **⋯** next to **Voice** — for its language, workspaces, what it remembers, notifications, **Clear conversation** and signing out of Santi. See [The Santi menu](/mobile/santi/#7-the-santi-menu).
