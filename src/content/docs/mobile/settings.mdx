The top toolbar (Header) and the expandable Menu bar (Sidebar) are where you access the system tools and settings of Santara AI Mobile.

## 1. Global Search (Workspace Search)

Tap the **"Magnifying Glass"** icon on the Header to open the quick search Modal. You can type any information:
- Guest name or phone number.
- Booking ID.
- Room or property name.

The system will scan all data in the workspace and return results instantly, helping you look up information without having to search through every page.

## 2. Expandable Menu Bar (Sidebar)

When you tap the **"More"** Tab or the Menu icon, a Sidebar will slide out providing the main controls:

### Switch Workspace
If you manage multiple branches or work for multiple hosts (Multi-Tenant), you can tap the current Workspace Name to select and jump to another Workspace. All data will be refreshed immediately.
You can also view your current Role in that Workspace (e.g., Owner, Manager, Cleaner).

### Change Currency and Theme
- **Currency Converter:** Flexibly switch between currencies (USD, VND, IDR...) to view revenue reports as you wish.
- **Theme:** Turn Light Mode and Dark Mode on/off depending on lighting conditions or personal preference.

### Sign Out
Tap the Sign Out button to safely log out of the app (a confirmation popup will appear).

## 3. Detailed Settings

From the Sidebar, you can access the **Settings** screen. Here, the application provides 2 main operational configurations:

### Workspace Settings
This is where you manage your business identity information. You can change the Workspace Name and Update the Avatar (Logo).
All changes in this area are global – meaning the entire staff logging into this Workspace will see the new name and logo immediately.

![Workspace Settings](/screens/en/workspace.en.png)

### Message Templates
To speed up responses and ensure professionalism, you can set up pre-composed message templates (e.g., Check-in Instructions, House Rules, WiFi Password).
Simply create a template once here, and when chatting with guests, you and your staff can pull these templates out and send them with just 2 taps instead of typing from scratch.

![Message Templates](/screens/en/template.en.png)
