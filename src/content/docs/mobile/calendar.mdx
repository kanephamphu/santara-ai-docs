The **Calendar** tab displays a unified grid of all bookings from Airbnb, Booking.com, and direct sources. This is where you grasp the room availability and occupancy rate of the entire system at a glance.

## 1. View Modes

The application supports flexible switching between 2 viewing modes to suit your needs:
- **Day View:** Displays detailed room status for a specific day.
- **Listing View:** Displayed as a horizontal Timeline. The color blocks represent a stay (Booking). The horizontal axis is time, the vertical axis is the room list.

![Unified Calendar on Mobile](/screens/en/calendar.en.png)

## 2. Listings Panel

If you have too many rooms, the calendar can get cluttered. You can tap the **"Listings shown"** button (eye icon) to open the selection panel. Here, you can tick to show only specific rooms or properties on the calendar, and hide unnecessary ones.

## 3. Smart Filters

Tap the **"Filters"** button to quickly filter the currently displayed bookings:
- **By Source/Channel:** View only guests from Airbnb, or only direct bookings.
- **By Upsell Opportunity:** Filter out customers with potential for service upgrades to prioritize care.

## 4. Moving Through Dates

- **Day** view: the **‹** and **›** arrows move one day at a time. Tap the date between them to come back to today.
- **Listing** view: the **‹** and **›** arrows beside the month name move a month at a time, into past stays or future ones. When you are away from the current month, a **Today** button appears — tap it to jump back.

The calendar loads dates as you go, so you can go as far back or ahead as you like, even with many rooms. If a small spinner shows next to the month name, those dates are still loading; give it a moment.

## 5. View Details and Quick Actions

When you **tap a color block (Booking)** on the calendar, the application will immediately open a detailed interface to view the guest's name, number of nights, payment status, and revenue.

![Booking Details (Listing)](/screens/en/listing.en.png)

From the booking you can **Edit** it, **Cancel booking**, **Open the message thread** or **See the clean**. If you have connected Santi, an **Ask Santi** button on the booking hands it to Santi to look into — see [Santi — Your AI Agent](/mobile/santi/).

To act on dates rather than a booking, select dates in **Listing** view: from **Selected dates** you can start a **New reservation** or open **Rates & availability** to change the price, minimum stay or **Stop sell** for those nights.
