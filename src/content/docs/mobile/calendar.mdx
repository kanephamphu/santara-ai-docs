Bookings from Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and your direct channel come together in one place. The **Calendar** tab shows which rooms are taken, the **Reservations** list shows every stay in order, and both open the same **booking details**.

## 1. Calendar

The **Calendar** tab has two views: **Day** and **Listing**.

### Day view

**Day** lists every room for one date. Each room shows **Free**, or the stay in it — **Staying** with the guest's name and the channel icon.

![Day view: every room and whether it is free or taken today](/screens/en/calendar.en.png)

### Listing view

**Listing** shows one listing as a month calendar. Pick the listing at the top, then read the month:

- the price for each free night,
- each booking as a bar with the guest's name and channel icon,
- a summary line, for example *4 of 31 nights booked · $666.1 revenue*.

![Listing view: one listing's month, with nightly prices and bookings](/screens/en/listing.en.png)

### Listings shown

If you have many rooms, tap **Listings shown** (the eye icon, with a count such as **6/6**) and tick only the rooms or properties you want on the calendar.

### Filters

Tap **Filters** to narrow the bookings on screen:

- **By source/channel:** only Airbnb guests, or only direct bookings.
- **By upsell opportunity:** guests worth offering an upgrade.

### Moving through dates

- **Day** view: the **‹** and **›** arrows move one day at a time. Tap the date between them to come back to today.
- **Listing** view: the **‹** and **›** arrows beside the month name move a month at a time, into past stays or future ones. **Today** takes you back to the current month.

The calendar loads dates as you go, so you can go as far back or ahead as you like, even with many rooms. If a small spinner shows next to the month name, those dates are still loading; give it a moment.

### Selected dates

To act on dates rather than a booking, select dates in **Listing** view: from **Selected dates** you can start a **New reservation** or open **Rates & availability** to change the price, minimum stay or **Stop sell** for those nights.

## 2. Reservations

### Reservations list

On a phone, open **More** → **Reservations**. On a tablet, **Reservations** is a tab of its own. The list is every stay in the workspace:

- **Search guest, property, or channel** finds a stay by any of the three.
- **All channels** narrows the list to one channel; the number is how many stays match.
- **Sort** orders the list by **Check-in date** (the default), **Check-out date**, **Revenue**, **Guest name**, **Property** or **Status**; the button beside it switches between ascending and descending.
- Four tabs, each with a count: **Upcoming**, **In house**, **Past** and **Cancelled**.

Each row shows the guest, the property and room, the dates and nights, the channel icon, the amount, and the status — **Confirmed** or **Pending**. Tap a row to open its [booking details](#3-booking-details).

![The Reservations list, with search, channel filter, sort and status tabs](/screens/en/reservations.en.png)

### Add reservation

**+ Add reservation** sits at the top of both **Calendar** and **Reservations**. Use it to enter a booking by hand, such as a guest who booked by phone.

## 3. Booking Details

Tap a booking on the calendar, or a row in **Reservations**, and its details slide up:

- **Phone**, **Property**, **Dates**, **Nights**, **Guests**
- **Payout** — what the stay pays you
- **Channel** and **Status**

![Booking details: the guest, the stay, the payout and the channel](/screens/en/booking.en.png)

Below the details:

- **Open the message thread** — the conversation with this guest in **Messages**.
- **See the clean** — the cleaning task for this stay in **Tasks**.
- **Ask Santi** at the top, if you have connected Santi, hands the booking to Santi to look into — see [Santi — Your AI Agent](/mobile/santi/).

A booking that came from a channel shows where it was made, for example *Booked on Vrbo — change or cancel it there*: change or cancel it on that channel. A direct booking can be changed here with **Edit** or **Cancel booking**.
