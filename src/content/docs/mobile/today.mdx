The **Today** tab is the home screen of the Santara AI Mobile app. It tells you what needs you today and how the next days look, without opening every booking.

Switch between two views with the tabs at the top: **Brief** and **Overview**.

## 1. Brief

**Brief** is the AI's read of your day. The headline counts what needs you — for example *8 things need you today* — above the date, the portfolio, your listing count and time zone, and a line of **arrivals**, **departures** and **turnovers**.

![Brief: what needs you today, starting with the most urgent](/screens/en/daily.en.png)

- **Share** opens a sheet to send today's brief by email, SMS or chat (WhatsApp, Zalo, Messenger, Telegram). Recipients need a Santara account with access to the workspace to open the link.
- **Regenerate** asks the AI to write the brief again from the latest data.

### What needs you

The items are grouped by how soon they matter:

- **Needs you now** — time-boxed: a door code that does not work, an AC that stopped cooling. Each card shows its type (Access, Maintenance…), its severity such as **Critical**, what happened, who is on it, and an **Open** button that takes you to it.
- **Today** — tied to a check-in or checkout.
- **For information** — no deadline.

### Further down

- **Upsell Opportunities** — guests worth offering an early check-in, a late checkout or another extra, with a draft message to copy.
- **Today's numbers** — revenue, occupancy and average nightly rate, with a workload gauge.
- **Tomorrow** — what is coming so the team can prepare.
- **Cleaning** — the day's cleans and how far along they are.
- **Mark reviewed** at the bottom — tap it once you have been through the day.

## 2. Overview

**Overview** is *Your hosting at a glance*: the date, your listings and units, and four cards. Choose the range with **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Next 7 days**.

![Overview: check-ins, check-outs, turnovers and stays for the range you choose](/screens/en/overview.en.png)

- **Check-ins** — guests arriving, with the date and time.
- **Check-outs** — guests leaving, with the date and time.
- **Turnovers** — rooms that need a clean between one guest and the next.
- **Staying** — guests in house now, with the day they leave.

Each card shows a count and the first few names; **+N more** says how many are left.
