The **Santara AI Mobile** app brings the day-to-day side of Santara AI to your phone: check-ins, guest messaging, cleaning, and issue resolution.

## 1. Getting the app

- **Android:** the app is live on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ai.santara.mobile).
- **iPhone:** not released yet. Until it is, use the web app in your phone browser — see [On your phone](/setup/mobile/).

It is the same account, the same workspaces and the same permissions as the web app.

## 2. Logging into the System

On the sign-in screen:

- **Sign in:** Enter your email and password to reach your workspace.
- **Show/Hide password:** Tap the eye icon in the password field to check what you typed.

![Login Screen](/screens/en/login.en.png)

## 3. Forgot Password (Recovery)

If you cannot remember your password, tap **Forgot password?** below the password field. Enter your email and tap **Send reset code**: a six-digit code arrives by email and works for 15 minutes. Enter it with your new password.

## 4. Creating an Account or Joining a Team

- **Starting your own workspace:** create your account on [santara.ai](https://www.santara.ai/) first, then sign in to the app with the same email and password. The app does not create accounts — the sign-in screen says so under the **Sign in** button.
- **Invited to someone's team:** open the invitation link they sent you (by email, WhatsApp or Zalo). It opens a join page in your browser: set a password — or enter your existing one — and you are in. A link that came by message also asks for a 6-digit code sent to your email. Then sign in to the app with that same email and password. See [Getting in](/start/invited-cleaner/#getting-in).

## 5. Handling Security States

If a sign-in fails, the app tells you why:

- **Invalid email or password.** The email or password does not match.
- **Please verify your email before signing in.** Check your inbox for the verification code first.
- **Account locked due to too many failed attempts. Try again in 15 minutes.** Wait, or reset your password.
- **This account has been deleted or is waiting to be deleted.** See [Deleting your account](#8-deleting-your-account).

## 6. Why You See Fewer Tabs

The tab bar has up to five tabs — **Today**, **Tasks**, **Calendar**, **Messages** and **More** — and each one appears only if your role is allowed to use it:

| Tab | Shown when your role can… |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Always shown |
| **Tasks** | See assigned cleans |
| **Calendar** | Read the calendar |
| **Messages** | Read guest messages |
| **More** | Always shown — opens the menu |

So a **cleaner** sees only **Today**, **Tasks** and **More**: no calendar, no guest inbox. That is the role working as intended, not a missing feature. Inside **Tasks**, the **Default cleaners** tab and **Skip cleaning task** are also for managers only. Roles are set by the workspace owner — see [Team and roles](/setup/team/).

## 8. Deleting Your Account

Accounts are closed on the web, not in the app. Sign in at [santara.ai/delete-account](https://www.santara.ai/delete-account/) and type your email to confirm.

- You are signed out on every device straight away. Your name, email, phone, photo and password are deleted within 30 days.
- Bookings and invoices stay with the workspace, without your details.
- If you are the only owner of a workspace other people use, make someone else an owner first.
- Changed your mind? Email support@santara.ai before the date shown.
