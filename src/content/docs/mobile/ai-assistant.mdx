Santara AI not only analyzes data in the background but also provides a direct communication tool: **Ask Santara Assistant**.

## Floating Action Button (FAB)

On the main screens of the application, you will always see a **Floating Action Button (FAB)** containing the AI icon located at the bottom corner of the screen.
Whether you are checking the calendar, viewing tasks, or managing settings, Ask Santara Assistant is always ready there to accompany you.

![AI Assistant - Ask Santara](/screens/en/AI_en.jpg)

## Use Cases

When you tap this FAB button, the AI chat interface will open. You can converse with it like a real junior manager:

- **Q&A and Quick Lookup:** You can ask *"What was the revenue of property A last week?"* or *"How many guests are checking in early today?"*, and the AI will answer immediately.
- **Data Analysis Requests:** The AI can access data deep within your Workspace. Instead of having to look at complex reports, you simply command the AI to synthesize and provide insights.
- **Quick Task Processing:** The AI can help you quickly create a ticket, pre-compose a message, or search for information about a past guest based on what you type.

With Ask Santara, your phone becomes a truly smart control center.
